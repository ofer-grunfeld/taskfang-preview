Privacy policy

Short version: TaskFANG apps process your files locally and do not upload them about you or your files. Your files stay on your PC. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The TaskFANG apps

  • No data collection. The apps contain no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • No uploads. TaskFANG never uploads your files or information about them. All processing happens on your computer.
  • No account. You never sign up or sign in to use a TaskFANG app.
  • No connection to TaskFANG servers. The apps do not contact us. A web page opens only when you click a link yourself, for example in the About window.

What is stored on your PC

To work properly, an app keeps local data in your own Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\TaskFANG). TaskFANG never sends them anywhere by itself:

  • Settings, such as the last folder you used and the window size.
  • A local log that helps diagnose problems. It may contain names of files and folders you worked with. Log files are deleted automatically after 14 days. You can choose to share a log with us when asking for help.

PST search acceleration

PST PDF Extractor can store encrypted acceleration data on this PC, including message dates, subjects, correspondent addresses and attachment names. This information supports faster searches and is not uploaded by TaskFANG. Final retention and removal instructions will be confirmed before release.

Files in the cloud (OneDrive)

If you choose to include online-only OneDrive files, Windows and OneDrive download them to your PC before the app reads them. That download is done by OneDrive under Microsoft's terms. TaskFANG itself still does not upload anything.

Purchases

TaskFANG apps are sold through the Microsoft Store. Payment and licensing are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details. Microsoft may share limited sales information with us as the publisher, such as the country of purchase.

The feedback form

If you send a suggestion or problem report through the feedback form, your message (and your e-mail address, only if you choose to give one) is sent by e-mail to the TaskFANG team. Nothing is stored on the website. We use it only to improve our tools and, if you asked, to reply to you. We never sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed. The e-mail is delivered through Cloudflare, which hosts this site.

This website

  • No cookies and no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • No external fonts or scripts. Everything is served from taskfang.com.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks, under its own privacy policy. We do not use this data to track visitors.
