Who it's for

People who need invoices, statements and other PDF attachments from an Outlook mail archive.

What it does

  • Filter by date range, correspondent, direction, filename, subject and mail folder.
  • Review results and copy selected PDF attachments to your chosen folder.
  • Use local acceleration data for faster repeat searches.

The app opens the PST read-only and does not modify or delete its contents.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Choose your PST archive.
  2. Set a date range and any additional filters.
  3. Search and review the matching PDFs.
  4. Select files and choose a destination folder.
  5. Copy your selection and review the result.

Understanding the results

Results identify matching PDF attachments. Copies are saved separately; password-protected PDFs remain protected. Search and copy can report completion, problems or interruption.

Questions

Can I use it while Outlook is using the PST?

The app yields access when Outlook needs the file. Close Outlook before searching that PST again.

Does it print or remove duplicates?

No. This edition finds and copies PDF attachments.

Requirements

Windows x64. Final supported Windows versions will be confirmed at release. OST and Outlook 97–2002 PST files are not supported. Final screenshots and the illustrated guide will follow acceptance of the app.
